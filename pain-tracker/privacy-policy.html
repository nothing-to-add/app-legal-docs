Privacy Policy - Pain Tracker

Last Updated: January 29, 2023
Quick Summary: Pain Tracker stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, share, or transmit any of your personal health information to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

Pain Tracker collects and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Health Data: Pain entries including intensity levels (1-10), pain locations, pain types, dates, and optional notes
  • Personal Information: Name, gender, and birth date (for personalization and PDF reports)
  • Usage Data: App preferences and settings

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Tracking and displaying your pain patterns
  • Generating personalized PDF reports
  • Providing statistical insights about your pain history
  • Personalizing your app experience

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only: All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

Your data is protected by:

  • Device-level encryption (iOS built-in security)
  • No network transmission of sensitive data
  • Local database storage only

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data. Since all data is stored locally, we have no access to your information and cannot share it with anyone.

5. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export: Generate PDF reports of your data
  • Delete: Remove individual entries or all data through the app
  • Access: View all your stored information within the app

6. Medical Disclaimer

Important: Pain Tracker is for informational purposes only and is not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with qualified healthcare providers regarding your health concerns.

7. Children's Privacy

Pain Tracker is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: nothing-to-add@outlook.com
  • Support Page: Pain Tracker Support

This privacy policy reflects our commitment to protecting your health data and maintaining your privacy.
