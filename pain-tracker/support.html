❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is my health data secure?
A: Yes! All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure SwiftData framework. We never transmit or store your data on external servers.
Q: Can I export my pain tracking data?
A: Absolutely! You can generate PDF reports of your pain data for any date range and share them with healthcare providers.
Q: How do I delete my data?
A: You can delete individual pain entries by swiping left in the history view, or delete all data through the Settings menu in User Details.
Q: What iOS versions are supported?
A: Pain Tracker requires iOS 17.6 or later and is optimized for modern iPhone models.
Q: Is this app a medical device?
A: No, Pain Tracker is for informational and tracking purposes only. It's not intended to diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical advice.
Q: Can I sync data between devices?
A: Currently, data is stored locally on each device. Cloud sync features are planned for future releases.

📱 Getting Started

New to Pain Tracker? Here's how to get started:

  1. Set up your profile in User Details (optional but recommended)
  2. Tap "Add Pain Entry" to record your first pain experience
  3. View your history and patterns in the History tab
  4. Generate reports in the Export section
