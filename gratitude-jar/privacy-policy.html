Privacy Policy - Gratitude Jar

📝 This app is currently in concept phase. Privacy policy subject to updates during development.
Last Updated: January 29, 2023
Quick Summary: Gratitude Jar stores all your gratitude entries locally on your device. We don't collect, share, or transmit any of your personal thoughts or entries to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

Gratitude Jar collects and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Gratitude Entries: Your written gratitude notes, photos, and timestamps
  • Personal Preferences: App settings, themes, and reminder preferences
  • Usage Patterns: Streak data and entry frequency (stored locally)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Storing and displaying your gratitude entries
  • Tracking your gratitude practice streaks
  • Providing reflection and review features
  • Personalizing your app experience

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage Only: All your gratitude entries and personal thoughts are stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

Your data is protected by:

  • Device-level encryption (iOS built-in security)
  • No network transmission of personal content
  • Local database storage only

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your data. Your gratitude entries and personal thoughts remain completely private and are never shared with anyone.

5. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Export: Export your gratitude entries for backup
  • Delete: Remove individual entries or all data through the app
  • Access: View all your stored gratitude entries within the app

6. Photos and Media

If you choose to add photos to your gratitude entries:

  • Photos are stored locally with your entries
  • We may request photo library access for this feature
  • Photos are never uploaded or shared externally

7. Children's Privacy

Gratitude Jar is designed to be family-friendly but is not specifically intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app develops. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

9. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: nothing-to-add@outlook.com
  • Support Page: Gratitude Jar Support

This privacy policy reflects our commitment to keeping your personal gratitude practice completely private.
