🌟 Planned Features

Core Features (Planned):

  • Daily Gratitude Entries - Write what you're grateful for each day
  • Photo Memories - Add photos to your gratitude entries
  • Streak Tracking - Build and maintain your gratitude practice
  • Reflection View - Review past entries and memories
  • Beautiful Themes - Customize your gratitude jar appearance
  • Export & Backup - Keep your gratitude journal safe
  • Gentle Reminders - Optional notifications to maintain your practice

Advanced Features (Under Consideration):

  • Gratitude insights and patterns
  • Mood tracking alongside gratitude
  • Gratitude sharing with loved ones (optional)
  • Voice notes for gratitude entries
  • Seasonal gratitude challenges

🎯 Our Vision

Gratitude Jar will be a beautifully designed, privacy-first app for cultivating daily gratitude habits.

Core Principles:

  • Privacy First: All data stored locally on your device
  • Beautiful Design: Inspiring and calming interface
  • Simple & Focused: Easy-to-use gratitude practice
  • No Pressure: Gentle encouragement, not guilt
